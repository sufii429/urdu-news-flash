# Urdu News Flash — اردو نیوز فلیش

TikTok-style vertical Urdu news app — scroll through the day's top Urdu headlines as full-screen cards, with a news sting and background music.

**Sources:** Independent Urdu · Express News · BBC Urdu · ARY Urdu · 24 News · BOL News
